Privacy

When enabled, this form sends your request type, message, optional reply email and a random request ID to this fan website’s existing Supabase backend. They are used only to review and respond to corrections, attribution concerns and removal requests. Submissions are not public; only authorized site owners and administrators may retrieve them.

No visitor account is required. This form does not use analytics, cookies or browser storage. Supabase and GitHub may process technical connection information under their own service policies. Requests are removed from the active inbox after 90 days during service use or earlier by an administrator; provider backups and technical logs follow provider retention policies.

To request earlier deletion, send a removal request identifying your earlier message without adding sensitive information. Replies are manual, only when a reply address is supplied; this form does not send automatic email.
