Factions of Zalesye

BANDITS

Bandits

Violent raiders who attack anyone for supplies. Disorganized at first glance, but capable of forming large squads. Their largest base lies near the highway at Svarog Factories. Encounters are always kill-or-be-killed.

MUDLARKS

Mudlarks

A half-mutated tribe resistant to Mist, treating it like a deity. They work near Lake Pavlie, hired to dispose of corpses so they don’t attract Mist. Misunderstood and eerie, their rituals grow more extreme as the world worsens.

MECHANISTS

Mechanists

Engineers and researchers who maintain tools, filters, and machinery for everyone else. Based around Sontse Station. Their technology keeps Zalesye alive, even if their prices and attitudes frustrate many.

RADA

Rada

A cooperative of farmers and traders promoting shared labour and comfort. They run farms and hire Palatines for protection. Their largest settlement, Dubravka Camp, attracts many Commonfolk. Their rise in influence comes with rumours of political manipulation and hired sabotage.

GUNNERS

Gunners

A ruthless gang running protection rackets and a growing drug trade. They control the Water Treatment plant and push into Palatine territory. Leaders act diplomatic, but payments are enforced with brutal efficiency.

COMMONFOLK

Commonfolk

Ordinary people of Zalesye who belong to no faction. Many farm, trade, or simply struggle to survive in the Mire. Beaten down by hardship, they often lack the means to change their fate.

ACOLYTES

Acolytes

Religious travellers maintaining churches, hospitals, and shelters. They spread pre-war beliefs and compete with Rada for the trust of Commonfolk. Their faith promises salvation, though many doubt such hope still matters.

PALATINES

Palatines

A military force formed from remnants of war. They guard Arcadia and the Bastion wall, keeping mutants out. Large but undisciplined, corruption and violence plague their ranks as they lose ground to the Gunners.

VULTURES

Vultures

Scavengers and roamers who survive by picking through the ruins of Zalesye. Not a unified group, more a loose alliance. They trade scraps, clear Bandits, and follow grim mantras to endure the wasteland’s monotony.
