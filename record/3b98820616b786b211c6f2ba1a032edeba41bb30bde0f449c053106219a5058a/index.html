Weapons

An incredibly high-caliber weapon capable of exploding its targets from a distance. Hard to maintain and not yet battle-tested, but the pure firepower makes up for any surprises. This pristine, scoped version could be used to snipe a chopper out of the sky. Maybe that's what it was built for in the War.
