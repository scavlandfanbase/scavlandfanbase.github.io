Dusty Overcoat

Gear / Armour & Clothing

A reliable dust coat is an important part of a Scavenger’s attire, since it protects against the unpredictable weather, dust and debris of the areas near the Thresholds. Although there is not much variety to be found in this category, it’s still very important to keep one’s dust coat relatively clean and in one piece.
